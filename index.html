Common questions.

NaijaAssets is an education platform offering live classes, recorded lessons, and affordable subscriptions for Nigerian students. Our curriculum is aligned with WAEC, NECO, and JAMB.

Subscriptions are billed monthly and auto-renew. Plans include Subject Subscription (one subject), Rex (recordings for subscribed subjects), Rex Pro (all recordings for your grade), and Vault Pass (unlimited access to every recording).

Students create educational content like memes, short videos, and UGC, and earn based on measurable impact.

We occasionally offer promotional periods. Check our pricing page for current offers. Your account remains active even after subscription expiration.

We use enterprise-grade security, encryption, and strict access controls. Our Privacy Policy and Security Policy detail our data handling practices. We never sell personal information.
