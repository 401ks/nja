About NaijaAssets

We are building Africa's most accessible AI-first learning platform - where every student has the tools, teachers, and support to succeed.

Start Learning Our Mission

Make high-quality education affordable, engaging and accessible.

We believe every child deserves access to world-class learning - regardless of their family's income or location.

NaijaAssets was founded in 2025 to close the education gap in Nigeria and across Africa. By combining live teaching with AI-powered tools, we deliver a learning experience that is personal, scalable, and affordable. Our platform serves students from primary through secondary school, with a curriculum that adapts to each learner's pace and style.

Education reimagined for the AI age.

We combine the best of human teaching with cutting-edge technology.

Qualified Teachers

Experienced educators deliver live lessons, provide feedback, and guide every student's learning journey.

AI-First Platform

AI Tutors, AI Whiteboards, and AI Voice provide personalised on-demand assistance beyond the classroom.

Recorded Lessons

Every live class is recorded and available on-demand. Review lessons anytime, anywhere.

Community Learning

Students learn together in small groups, building confidence, collaboration skills, and lasting friendships.

Serving students across Africa and beyond.

Starting with Nigeria and expanding across the continent.
