# About NaijaAssets

NaijaAssets exists to make high-quality education affordable, engaging and accessible for students. We combine live teaching, recorded lessons, artificial intelligence, interactive learning, technology, community, rewards, and scholarships into one unified learning platform.

## Mission
Make high-quality education affordable, engaging and accessible for students.

## Target Markets
- Primary: Nigeria
- Secondary: Africa
- Tertiary: International students worldwide

## Our Approach
- AI-first learning platform
- Live classes with direct teacher interaction
- Recorded lessons for flexible study
- Community-driven content and rewards
- Scholarship opportunities for deserving students

## Long-Term Vision
- AI Teachers and AI Tutors
- AI Whiteboards for visual learning
- AI Voice conversations
- Adaptive learning paths
- Mobile and desktop applications
- Enterprise and school integrations
