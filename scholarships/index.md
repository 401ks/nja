# Scholarships — NaijaAssets

NaijaAssets offers scholarship programs to support outstanding students. Scholarships may include academic scholarships, merit awards, competitions, community initiatives, partner-funded scholarships, and referral scholarships.

## Types
- Academic scholarships — based on performance and potential
- Merit awards — recognizing excellence
- Competitions — win through contests and challenges
- Community initiatives — supporting deserving students
- Partner-funded — sponsored by partner organizations
- Referral scholarships — through the referral program

## Important Notes
- Scholarships may be modified or discontinued
- Selection decisions are final
- Full terms available in the Scholarship Terms
