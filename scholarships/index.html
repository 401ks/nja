Scholarships - Merit. Opportunity. Access.

NaijaAssets believes every talented student deserves the chance to succeed. Our scholarship program rewards academic excellence, creative achievement, and community spirit.

Opportunities for every path.

We offer a range of scholarships to support students at every stage of their learning journey.

Academic Scholarships

Awarded to students who demonstrate outstanding academic performance and consistent dedication to their studies.

Merit Awards

Special recognition for students who go above and beyond - top performers in assessments, projects, and class participation.

Competitions

Win scholarships through academic contests, innovation challenges, and skill-based competitions held throughout the year.

Community Initiatives

Partner-funded programs and referral-based scholarships that extend access to underserved and deserving students.

Partner-Funded

Scholarships sponsored by partner organizations committed to expanding educational access across Africa.

Referral Scholarships

Earn scholarship credit by referring friends who enroll. The more students you help, the more you save.

Four steps to your scholarship.

1

Apply

Submit your application through the scholarship portal. Provide your academic records and personal statement.

2

Submit Documents

Upload required documents - transcripts, recommendation letters, or project portfolios depending on the scholarship type.

3

Review

Our scholarship committee reviews all applications. Shortlisted candidates may be invited for an interview or assessment.

4

Awarded

Successful applicants receive their scholarship award, which is applied toward their NaijaAssets subscription or learning materials.
