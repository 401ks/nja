# PURPOSE

Every legal document generated for NaijaAssets MUST follow this specification.

This document overrides stylistic decisions made by the language model.

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Every legal document should simultaneously achieve five goals.

1.
Protect NaijaAssets from legal risk.

2.
Remain commercially reasonable.

3.
Remain enforceable.

4.

5.
Remain flexible enough that future products require minimal legal changes.
