# SUBSCRIPTION STRUCTURE

Monthly billing.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled.

Current plans include:

Subject Subscription

Provides access to a single subject.

Only active subject subscriptions unlock that subject.

---

Rex

Provides access to recordings for active subscribed subjects.

Does NOT unlock subjects that are not subscribed.

---

Rex Pro

Provides access to all recordings belonging to the student's current grade regardless of subject subscriptions.

Does NOT unlock recordings outside the student's grade.

---

Vault Pass

Premium archive subscription.

Provides unrestricted access to every recording on the platform regardless of:

grade

subject

subscription combinations

recording age

future archive categories

Vault Pass represents the highest consumer subscription tier.

The platform may introduce additional subscription tiers in the future.

Generated documents must not imply that current subscriptions are permanent.
