Earn While You Share

The NaijaAssets Affiliate Program is one of many ways to grow with us. When students you refer sign up and subscribe, you earn commissions. Transparent tracking, recurring revenue, and a scalable opportunity built for Nigerian affiliates who believe in better education.

How it works

1

Sign Up

Create your NaijaAssets account and enroll in the Affiliate Program. No special requirements - anyone can join.

2

Get Your Link

Receive your unique referral link and marketing resources. Share it anywhere - social media, groups, or directly with students.

3

Refer Students

When students click your link and subscribe to a paid NaijaAssets plan, they're attributed to you. Full tracking with real-time dashboard.

4

Earn Commissions

Earn recurring commissions on every referred student's active subscription. The longer they stay, the more you earn.
