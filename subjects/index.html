Which subject should my child take?

Simple guidance to help you choose based on your child's academic needs.

Mathematics

Ideal for students who need stronger problem-solving skills, foundational clarity in numbers and algebra, and structured preparation for exams.

Basic / Integrated Science

Recommended for students who want to build scientific thinking, understand core concepts across Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, and improve lab skills.

Both Subjects

Enrolling in both Mathematics and Science is highly recommended for comprehensive academic growth, especially for students in SSS preparing for WAEC and JAMB.

Every class, built for engagement.

Consistent structure across every subject ensures quality and familiarity.

Frequency

Recordings

All classes are recorded and available on-demand within the platform for later review.
