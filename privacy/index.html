Privacy Policy

How NaijaAssets collects, uses, and protects information shared by parents and students.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

NaijaAssets values the privacy and trust of the parents and students who use our platform. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used to deliver our tutoring services, and how we protect your data. By using our website, contacting us, or enrolling in classes, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Our approach is simple: we only collect what we need to operate effectively, communicate clearly, and support your child's learning journey.

2. Information We May Collect

We collect information that is necessary to manage enrollment, schedule classes, and provide academic support. This may include:

  • Parent or guardian name
  • Student name
  • Student grade or class level
  • Subjects of interest
  • Phone number and WhatsApp contact details
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Scheduling preferences and class links
  • Messages sent through our website forms or WhatsApp

We do not collect sensitive personal information or data that is unnecessary for enrollment, scheduling, or academic support.

3. How Information Is Used

Information collected is used strictly for operational and educational purposes, including:

  • Managing class enrollment and student placement
  • Sending class schedules, reminders, and Google Meet links
  • Providing academic support and answering inquiries
  • Communicating with parents regarding schedules and updates
  • Organizing tutor assignments and class logistics
  • Improving our tutoring services and platform experience
  • Responding to administrative or support requests

4. Online Class Platforms & Third-Party Services

NaijaAssets relies on trusted third-party platforms to deliver live classes and maintain communication. These include:

  • Google Meet for live tutoring sessions
  • WhatsApp for scheduling, reminders, and parent support
  • Form and payment providers for enrollment inquiries and fee processing

Please note that some information may be processed through these platforms as part of normal operations. These services are governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage parents to review. NaijaAssets does not control the independent data practices of third-party services and is not liable for their separate policies.

5. Class Recordings & Media

In certain cases, live sessions may be recorded for educational review, tutor training, or quality assurance. We prioritize student comfort and do not require cameras to be enabled during lessons. Students may participate fully using audio only or the chat feature.

Any public promotional material featuring students will only be shared with explicit parental permission. When appropriate, identifying details may be obscured to protect privacy.

6. Data Sharing

We believe in complete transparency regarding data handling. Information may be shared only when strictly necessary to deliver our services, such as:

  • Assigned tutors for class coordination and scheduling
  • Technical platforms required for scheduling or payment processing
  • Legal compliance when required by law

NaijaAssets does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to unrelated third parties, advertisers, or external organizations.

7. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide using standard digital security practices and trusted service providers. Access to student and parent information is limited to authorized staff and tutors who require it for operational purposes.

However, no online platform or digital communication channel can guarantee absolute security. We encourage users to protect their own devices, secure their accounts, and avoid sharing unnecessary personal details in public or unsecured environments.

8. Children & Student Privacy

NaijaAssets provides services to minors under the supervision of parents or legal guardians. We strongly recommend that parents remain involved during the enrollment process and monitor class participation.

Students are encouraged to focus on academic content and avoid sharing personal information beyond what is necessary for learning. Tutors are trained to maintain a safe, respectful, and education-focused environment at all times.

9. Contact & Support

If you have questions, requests, or concerns regarding privacy, your personal information, or how it is handled, please reach out through our official support channels:

  • Email: forms@naijaassets.com
  • WhatsApp: +234 903 411 0894
  • Web Form: Contact Page

We aim to respond to privacy-related inquiries transparently and within a few business days.

10. Policy Updates

As our services grow and operational needs evolve, this policy may be updated to reflect new practices, improved tools, or regulatory guidance. Significant changes will be communicated through our website or direct communication channels.

Continued use of our platform after updates are posted indicates acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage parents to review this page periodically for any changes.
