## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Privacy Policy have the meanings set forth in our Terms of Service.

**"Personal Information"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under applicable privacy laws.

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Information, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, disclosure, dissemination, or deletion.

**"Service Providers"** means third-party companies and individuals that process Personal Information on our behalf to facilitate or provide Services related to the Platform.

We collect Personal Information that you voluntarily provide when you:

- Complete your profile or account settings
- Submit assignments, questions, comments, or other Content
- Participate in scholarship applications, competitions, or rewards programs
- Apply to become a Teacher or Affiliate
- Communicate with us through support or feedback channels

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

- Payment processors (transaction confirmations, limited account details)
- Authentication providers (OAuth providers, if integrated)

- School or enterprise partners (where applicable)
