## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used in this Parental Consent Policy have the meanings set forth in the Terms of Service unless otherwise defined below.

**"Child"** or **"Minor"** means an individual under the age of 13, or such other age as determined by applicable law in the minor's jurisdiction.

**"Parent"** or **"Legal Guardian"** means the individual with legal authority to make decisions on behalf of a Minor.

**"Verifiable Parental Consent"** means any reasonable effort to ensure that a Parent is notified of the Platform's data practices and has authorized the Minor's use of the Platform, consistent with applicable law.

## 2. Age Policy Overview

### 2.1 Minimum Age

The Platform is not intended for Children under the age of 13 without Verifiable Parental Consent. If you are under 13, you may not use the Platform unless your Parent has provided consent in accordance with this Policy and applicable law.

### 2.2 Minors Aged 13 to 18

If you are between the ages of 13 and 18 (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction), you may use the Platform with the review and consent of your Parent or Legal Guardian, who must accept the Terms of Service on your behalf.

### 2.3 Jurisdictional Variations

Age requirements may vary by jurisdiction. We comply with applicable age-related laws in the jurisdictions where we operate.

## 3. When Parental Consent Is Required

### 3.1 Account Creation

Parental consent is required before a Child creates an account or submits Personal Information to the Platform.

### 3.2 Data Collection

We will not knowingly collect Personal Information from Children without Verifiable Parental Consent, except as permitted by applicable law.

### 3.3 AI Features

Where AI Features process Personal Information of Children, additional parental consent or notice may be required under applicable law.

## 4. Consent Process

### 4.1 Consent Methods

Verifiable Parental Consent may be obtained through one or more of the following methods, depending on legal requirements and technical feasibility:

- Electronic consent form signed by the Parent
- Credit card or payment verification
- Video call or phone verification
- Email verification with follow-up confirmation
- Government-issued identification verification (which will be deleted after verification)
- Knowledge-based authentication questions

### 4.2 Consent Content

The Parent will be informed of:

- The types of Personal Information collected from the Child
- How the information will be used, disclosed, and stored
- The Parent's rights regarding the Child's information
- How to revoke consent and request deletion of the Child's information

### 4.3 Consent Record

We will maintain records of Verifiable Parental Consent as required by applicable law.

## 5. Parental Rights

### 5.1 Rights of Parents

Parents have the following rights regarding their Child's account and Personal Information:

- Review their Child's Personal Information held by NaijaAssets
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
- Request deletion of their Child's Personal Information
- Refuse further collection or use of their Child's information
- Revoke consent at any time
- Request that their Child's account be suspended or terminated

### 5.2 Exercising Rights

To exercise parental rights, please contact us at legal@legal.naijaassets.com with:

- Your name and relationship to the Child
- Your Child's account information
- The specific right(s) you wish to exercise
- Documentation verifying your identity and parental authority

### 5.3 Response

We will respond to parental requests within the timeframe required by applicable law, or within a reasonable period if no specific timeframe is prescribed.

## 6. Verification

### 6.1 Parent Verification

We may take reasonable steps to verify that the person providing consent is the Child's Parent or Legal Guardian. Verification methods may vary depending on the sensitivity of the information and legal requirements.

### 6.2 Identity Verification

We may request documentation to verify the Parent's identity and legal authority. Such documentation will be used solely for verification purposes and will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

## 7. Revocation of Consent

### 7.1 Right to Revoke

A Parent may revoke their consent at any time. Revocation of consent will result in:

- Termination of the Child's ability to use Platform features that require the processing of Personal Information
- Deletion of the Child's Personal Information, subject to legal retention requirements
- Closure or restriction of the Child's account as appropriate

### 7.2 Effect of Revocation

Following revocation, the Child may no longer be able to access certain features or benefits of the Platform. Revocation does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before revocation.

### 7.3 Process

To revoke consent, Parents should contact legal@legal.naijaassets.com with their Child's account information and a clear statement of revocation.

## 8. Data Handling

### 8.1 Collection and Use

Personal Information collected from Children is used only for the purposes described in our Privacy Policy and the consent provided by the Parent. We do not use Children's Personal Information for behavioral advertising or marketing without parental consent.

### 8.2 Disclosure

We do not disclose Children's Personal Information to third parties except as necessary to provide the Platform, as required by law, or as authorized by the Parent.

### 8.3 Retention

Children's Personal Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the Platform services and comply with legal obligations. When consent is revoked or the information is no longer needed, it will be deleted in accordance with our data retention practices.

### 8.4 Security

Children's Personal Information is protected using the same security measures described in our Security Policy and Privacy Policy.
