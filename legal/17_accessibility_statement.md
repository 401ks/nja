## 1. Commitment to Accessibility

NaijaAssets is committed to providing a platform that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of ability, technology, or circumstance. We believe that educational technology should be inclusive and that accessibility is fundamental to our mission of making high-quality education affordable, engaging, and accessible for all students.

We are dedicated to ensuring that individuals with disabilities can perceive, understand, navigate, interact with, and contribute to the Platform.

## 2. Accessibility Standards

### 2.1 Reference Standards

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines define standards for making web content accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities.

### 2.2 Scope

This commitment applies to the NaijaAssets website, web application, and future mobile and desktop applications, to the extent technically feasible.

### 2.3 Ongoing Compliance

Accessibility compliance is an ongoing process. We continue to evaluate and improve the Platform to meet evolving accessibility standards and guidelines.

## 3. Accessibility Features

The Platform incorporates or will incorporate the following accessibility features:

### 3.1 Visual Accessibility

- Support for screen readers and assistive technologies
- Sufficient color contrast ratios for text and interactive elements
- Text alternatives for non-text content (where technically feasible)
- Resizable text without loss of functionality
- Keyboard-navigable interface elements
- Focus indicators for interactive elements

### 3.2 Auditory Accessibility

- Captions and subtitles for video content (where available)
- Transcripts for audio content (where available)
- Visual indicators for audio notifications and alerts

### 3.3 Interaction Accessibility

- Keyboard navigation throughout the Platform
- Adequate time to complete tasks and assessments
- Consistent navigation and identification of elements
- Error identification and suggestions for input correction

### 3.4 AI Accessibility

AI Features are designed with accessibility in mind, including:

- AI Voice interactions for students who benefit from auditory learning
- AI-powered captioning and transcription
- Adaptable AI interfaces that work with assistive technologies
- Future AI features to support diverse learning needs

## 4. Ongoing Efforts

### 4.1 Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing commitment. We continuously work to:

- Evaluate the Platform using automated accessibility testing tools
- Conduct manual accessibility audits and user testing
- Train our development team on accessible design and coding practices
- Incorporate accessibility requirements into our product development lifecycle
- Monitor and address accessibility issues reported by Users

### 4.2 Third-Party Components

Where the Platform incorporates third-party components, we work with providers to encourage accessibility compliance. However, we may have limited control over the accessibility of third-party content and services.

### 4.3 AI and Accessibility

We are committed to leveraging AI Features to enhance accessibility, including AI-powered assistive technologies, adaptive interfaces, and personalized accessibility settings.

## 5. Compatibility

### 5.1 Supported Technologies

The Platform is designed to be compatible with:

- Modern web browsers (including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge)
- Screen reader software (including JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack)
- Operating system accessibility features
- Keyboard-only navigation

### 5.2 Limitations

The Platform may not be fully accessible in all configurations, older browsers, or specialized environments. We strive to maximize compatibility within the constraints of our technology and resources.

## 6. Third-Party Content

### 6.1 User-Generated Content

Accessibility of User-generated Content (including Teacher Content, Student submissions, and community posts) is the responsibility of the content creator. We encourage Users to create accessible content but cannot guarantee the accessibility of all UGC.

### 6.2 Third-Party Services

Content or services provided by third parties and integrated with the Platform may have their own accessibility characteristics. We encourage third-party providers to maintain accessible services.

## 7. Feedback and Support

### 7.1 Reporting Issues

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of the Platform, please contact us. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or service through alternative means.

### 7.2 Feedback

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of the Platform. Please provide:

- A description of the accessibility issue
- The specific page or feature you encountered difficulty with
- Your assistive technology or browser configuration
- Any suggestions for improvement

### 7.3 Response Time

We will acknowledge your accessibility feedback promptly and work to address issues in a reasonable timeframe.
