## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the meanings set forth in the Terms of Service unless otherwise defined below.

**"Affiliate"** means a User who has enrolled in the Affiliate program and promotes the Platform through referral links.

**"Commission"** means the compensation payable to an Affiliate for Qualifying Referrals, as determined by the then-current commission structure.

**"Qualifying Referral"** means a new User who: (a) registers for the Platform through an Affiliate's unique referral link; (b) activates a paid Subscription; and (c) satisfies any additional conditions established by NaijaAssets.

**"Referral Link"** means a unique URL or code assigned to an Affiliate for tracking referrals.

## 2. Eligibility and Enrollment

### 2.1 Enrollment

The Affiliate program is open to Users who meet eligibility criteria established by NaijaAssets. Enrollment is subject to application and approval. Approval is at our sole discretion and may be withdrawn at any time.

### 2.2 Eligibility

To be eligible, you must:

- Have a valid account on the Platform
- Provide accurate enrollment information
- Comply with all applicable Platform policies
- Not be located in a restricted jurisdiction

### 2.3 Multiple Accounts

Affiliates may maintain only one Affiliate account. Multiple accounts for the same individual or entity are prohibited.

## 3. Referral Tracking

### 3.1 Referral Links

NaijaAssets provides unique Referral Links or codes to Affiliates. Commissions are tracked based on referrals made through these links.

### 3.2 Attribution

A Qualifying Referral is attributed to the Affiliate whose Referral Link was used during the referral process. NaijaAssets' attribution and tracking records are final and binding.

### 3.3 Cookie Duration

Referral tracking cookies or similar mechanisms may have a limited duration as specified in the Affiliate dashboard. Referrals outside this window may not be attributed.

### 3.4 Self-Referral Prohibition

Self-referrals are strictly prohibited. Affiliates may not use their own Referral Link to earn Commissions on their own Subscriptions or purchases.

## 4. Commissions

### 4.1 Commission Structure

Commission rates, structures, and calculation methods are communicated through the Affiliate dashboard or other mechanisms. The current commission structure may be modified at any time with notice.

### 4.2 Qualifying Transactions

Commissions are earned only on Qualifying Referrals that meet all conditions. We reserve the right to determine what constitutes a Qualifying Referral and to modify qualifying criteria.

### 4.3 Commissions on Future Purchases

Unless otherwise specified, Commissions may be limited to initial Qualifying Referrals or may extend to future Subscription renewals, as determined by the then-current commission structure.

### 4.4 No Commission Guarantee

NaijaAssets does not guarantee any minimum Commission, number of referrals, or revenue. Affiliate earnings depend entirely on the Affiliate's promotional efforts and platform conditions.

### 5.2 Payment Methods

### 5.3 Deductions

NaijaAssets may deduct applicable taxes, fees, or chargebacks from Commission payments as required by law or as specified in the commission structure.

### 5.5 No Guarantee

Payment of Commissions is contingent on NaijaAssets receiving payment from the referred User. Commissions on refunded, charged back, or fraudulent transactions will not be paid or will be deducted from future payments.
