Simple & Accessible Pricing

Structured online tutoring designed to remain accessible for Nigerian families while providing live academic support.

Pricing may vary depending on grade level, subjects, and class structure.

Our pricing is structured to be transparent and adaptable. Rather than rigid packages, final rates are determined based on:

  • Subjects selected and academic level
  • Class structure (group vs. private)
  • Weekly session frequency
  • Additional support or resource requirements

The goal is to keep structured online learning accessible without compromising the quality of tutor interaction or academic organization.

Subject & Grade Flexibility

Academic needs vary. To keep learning structured and affordable, pricing adjusts based on:

  • Academic Level: JSS and SSS curriculum requirements differ in complexity and session focus.
  • Subject Count: Enrolling in multiple subjects may qualify for bundled scheduling.
  • Session Frequency: Weekly session counts are tailored to student workload and exam timelines.
  • Learning Format: Private tutoring naturally involves different scheduling and resource allocation than group sessions.

During enrollment, admins will review your child's needs and provide a clear, upfront breakdown before any commitment is made.

How Payments Work

Enrollment is straightforward. Once subjects and schedules are confirmed, payment instructions are shared directly by our support team.

Currently, payments are processed securely through approved local channels including:

  • Direct bank transfers
  • Approved mobile payment methods
  • Other verified local transfer options

As our platform expands, we will integrate additional automated payment systems. You will always receive clear confirmation and receipts for all transactions.

Questions About Pricing

Quick answers to common concerns.

Can my child join multiple subjects?

Yes. Subject combinations are discussed during enrollment, and schedules are arranged to avoid overlap.

Do students need expensive devices?

No. Most classes can be attended using a standard smartphone with stable internet access. Laptops or tablets are helpful but not required.

Can pricing change later?

Pricing structures may evolve as programs expand or operational needs change. Enrolled students are typically given advance notice before any adjustments take effect.

Are there promotions or trial offers?

Occasional enrollment promotions or introductory trial periods may be announced through WhatsApp or our website.
