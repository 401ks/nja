# Pricing — NaijaAssets Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions for quality education. Cancel anytime.

## Plans
- **Subject Subscription** — Access to one subject. Live classes, recordings, AI tutoring.
- **Rex** — Recordings for actively subscribed subjects.
- **Rex Pro** (Popular) — All recordings for your current grade. No subject restrictions.
- **Vault Pass** — Unlimited access to every recording. No grade or subject limits.

## Billing
- Monthly billing, automatic renewal
- 5-day grace period for failed payments
- No refunds generally (exceptions at company discretion or where required by law)
- Payment processors include Stripe and future providers

## Features
- Live online classes
- Recorded lessons
- AI Tutor, Whiteboard, Voice, Vision
- Assignments and resources
- Student dashboard
- Teacher support
