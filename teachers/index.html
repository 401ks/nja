Clear terms for teachers.

We believe in transparency about how content is used on the platform. All teachers operate as independent contractors, not employees.

You Retain Ownership

You retain full ownership of your original teaching materials and lesson content. NaijaAssets does not claim ownership of your intellectual property.

Platform License

By teaching on NaijaAssets, you grant us the rights to record, store, edit, publish, reuse, market, monetize, archive, and process your educational content through AI systems. This license enables us to operate and grow the platform while you retain ownership of your materials.
