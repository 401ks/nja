# Teach on NaijaAssets

Independent teachers create live lessons, recordings, and educational content on the NaijaAssets platform.

## Who We're Looking For
- Qualified educators in Mathematics, Sciences, and other subjects
- Subject matter experts passionate about teaching
- Teachers who want to reach students across Africa

## How It Works
1. Apply through the platform
2. Interview and onboarding process
3. Set up your teaching profile
4. Create live classes and recorded content
5. Engage with students

## Key Information
- Teachers are independent contractors
- Teachers retain ownership of original teaching materials
- Teachers grant NaijaAssets rights to record, store, edit, publish, reuse, market, monetize, archive, and AI-process educational content
- Full terms in the Teacher Agreement
