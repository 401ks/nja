NaijaAssets is a digital real estate and asset listing platform.

It is designed to help users buy, sell, rent, and manage property-style assets through a centralized online marketplace.

CORE PURPOSE:
NaijaAssets connects users with available properties and listings in a structured and searchable system.

The platform focuses on making asset discovery simple, transparent, and accessible.

MAIN FEATURES:

1. Property Listings
- Users can browse available assets and listings
- Listings include residential, commercial, and rental properties
- Each listing contains details such as location, price, and availability
- NaijaAssets charge 500$ per listing when a house is listed on the platform

2. Buy / Rent / Sell System
- Users can list properties for sale or rent
- Buyers and renters can search and filter listings
- The system supports multiple property types including shortlets and long-term rentals

3. Search & Discovery
- Users can search properties by location, price range, and category
- Popular locations include major cities such as Lagos, Abuja, and other high-demand areas
- The platform supports both simple search and AI-assisted search queries

4. User Accounts
- Users can create accounts and manage their listings
- Registered users can track saved properties and inquiries
- Dashboard functionality allows listing management

5. Listing Management
- Property owners can upload and manage listings
- Listings can be updated or removed at any time
- Featured listings may be highlighted on the platform

6. Platform Mission
NaijaAssets aims to improve transparency in the real estate market by making property data easier to access and understand.

It focuses on reducing friction between buyers, sellers, and renters through a simple digital system.

7. Market Focus
The platform is modeled after modern real estate marketplaces, similar to major property listing websites.

It is optimized for scalability, searchability, and user-friendly browsing of assets.

CONTACT / SUPPORT:
Users can contact support via platform messaging or listed support email channels.
