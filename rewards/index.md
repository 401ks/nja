# Content Rewards — NaijaAssets

Students can earn rewards by creating educational content. This is one of our primary growth engines.

## How It Works
1. Create educational content (memes, short videos, UGC)
2. Submit through the platform
3. Content is reviewed and measured for impact
4. Earn rewards based on measurable engagement

## Guidelines
- Rewards are discretionary
- Approval is not guaranteed
- All content must comply with Acceptable Use Policy and Community Guidelines
- Full terms in the Rewards Program Terms
