Terms of Service

Guidelines, responsibilities, and conditions for using NaijaAssets services and online classes.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

These Terms of Service govern your access to and use of NaijaAssets tutoring services, online classes, communication channels, and website. By enrolling, participating in sessions, or contacting our support team, you agree to these terms.

Because many of our students are minors, parents or legal guardians are responsible for ensuring that their children understand and follow these guidelines while participating in our platform.

2. Services Provided

NaijaAssets provides structured academic support through:

  • Live online tutoring sessions via Google Meet
  • Small-group collaborative learning environments
  • Subject-focused academic support and guidance
  • Scheduling coordination and WhatsApp communication
  • Academic materials and structured learning frameworks

We focus on delivering consistent, organized educational support. We do not guarantee specific academic results, grade improvements, or examination outcomes, as these depend on multiple factors including student engagement, prior preparation, and external circumstances.

3. Class Participation Rules

To maintain a productive learning environment, we expect all participants to:

  • Join sessions on time and participate respectfully
  • Use appropriate names and professional communication
  • Avoid sharing class links, meeting IDs, or materials publicly
  • Keep cameras on only if comfortable, as tutors primarily require audio and chat participation

Disruptive behavior, inappropriate language, or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from sessions without notice.

4. Parent/Guardian Responsibilities

Parents and guardians play a key role in the success of their child's learning journey. By enrolling, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate enrollment and contact information
  • Ensure students have a suitable device and stable internet connection
  • Monitor student participation and communication channels
  • Support attendance and encourage consistent study habits

We encourage parents to maintain open communication with our admins regarding schedules, progress, or any concerns.

5. Payments & Enrollment

Access to NaijaAssets classes requires confirmed payment according to the active pricing structure. We reserve the right to:

  • Adjust pricing for future enrollment periods as needed
  • Restrict class access if payments are overdue or unverified
  • Offer trial sessions or promotional pricing under separate, clearly stated conditions

All fees are communicated clearly before enrollment. We will notify you in advance of any pricing changes that affect your current or future sessions.

6. Refunds & Missed Classes

We operate on a structured enrollment model designed for consistency. Refunds and rescheduling are handled as follows:

  • Missed classes due to personal absence, illness, or scheduling conflicts generally do not qualify for automatic refunds or credits
  • Refund requests are reviewed case-by-case and may be approved under exceptional circumstances
  • If NaijaAssets cancels or experiences significant technical disruptions, affected sessions will be rescheduled or credited where possible

We recommend confirming your schedule availability before enrollment to ensure consistent participation.

7. Online Platform & Technical Limitations

Our services rely on third-party platforms including Google Meet, WhatsApp, and payment processors. Please note:

  • Session quality may be affected by internet connectivity, device performance, or power interruptions
  • We are not responsible for outages, restrictions, or policy changes made by external platforms
  • Students and parents are responsible for maintaining functional devices and suitable network access

We will communicate promptly about any platform changes and provide alternative arrangements when technically feasible.

8. Recordings & Educational Materials

Some sessions may be recorded for quality assurance, tutor training, or student review. These recordings are for internal educational use only. Additionally:

  • Class materials, worksheets, and digital resources are provided exclusively for enrolled students
  • Redistribution, resale, or public sharing of proprietary materials is strictly prohibited
  • Recordings will not be published publicly without explicit parental consent

NaijaAssets provides educational support services designed to assist learning. We do not guarantee specific academic performance, examination scores, or admission outcomes. Participation in our services is at the user's discretion.

NaijaAssets shall not be held liable for:

  • Indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from service use

Our goal is to deliver reliable, structured tutoring while remaining transparent about the realistic limits of online educational support.

11. Account & Access Termination

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate access to NaijaAssets services in cases involving:

  • Repeated violation of class participation rules

Terminations will be communicated clearly. We aim to resolve conflicts fairly before taking restrictive actions.

Your use of NaijaAssets services is also governed by our Privacy Policy, which outlines how we collect, store, and protect personal information. By using our platform, you acknowledge and agree to both documents.
