1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used in these Scholarship Terms have the meanings set forth in the Terms of Service unless otherwise defined below.

"Scholarship" means a financial award, discount, grant, or other benefit granted to an eligible Student to support their education on the Platform.

"Scholarship Program" means any specific scholarship initiative operated by NaijaAssets, which may include academic scholarships, merit awards, competitions, community initiatives, partner-funded scholarships, referral scholarships, and other programs.

"Selection Criteria" means the criteria established for a particular Scholarship Program, which may include academic performance, merit, financial need, competition results, community involvement, or other factors determined by NaijaAssets.

2. Scholarship Programs

2.1 Program Descriptions

NaijaAssets may offer various Scholarship Programs. Each program will have its own specific terms, which will be published at the time of application. Current and future Scholarship Programs may include:

  • Academic scholarships based on performance or achievement
  • Merit awards recognizing exceptional talent or contribution
  • Competitions with scholarship prizes
  • Community initiatives supporting educational access
  • Partner-funded scholarships sponsored by third-party organizations
  • Referral scholarships for referring qualified applicants

2.2 Program-Specific Terms

Each Scholarship Program may have additional terms, conditions, eligibility requirements, and deadlines. In the event of a conflict between these Scholarship Terms and program-specific terms, the program-specific terms govern for that particular program.

3. Eligibility

3.1 General Eligibility

Eligibility requirements vary by Scholarship Program and will be specified in program materials. General requirements may include:

  • Active registration on the Platform
  • Age, grade, or academic level requirements
  • Residency or nationality requirements
  • Enrollment in specified Subscriptions or courses
  • Compliance with Platform policies

3.2 Verification

Applicants must provide accurate and verifiable information. NaijaAssets reserves the right to verify eligibility, academic status, and any information provided in applications.

3.3 Ineligibility

The following individuals are generally ineligible unless otherwise specified:

  • Employees, contractors, and affiliates of NaijaAssets
  • Immediate family members of NaijaAssets personnel
  • Individuals in restricted jurisdictions
  • Individuals who have previously been suspended or terminated from the Platform

4. Application Process

4.1 Application Submission

Applications must be submitted through the designated process by the specified deadline. Late or incomplete applications may not be considered.

4.2 Application Materials

Depending on the Scholarship Program, required materials may include:

  • Personal and academic information
  • Essays, written statements, or personal narratives
  • Videos, presentations, or creative submissions
  • Academic records, transcripts, or certificates
  • Letters of recommendation or references
  • Projects, portfolios, or work samples
  • Financial information (where applicable)

4.3 No Application Guarantee

Submission of an application does not guarantee consideration, award, or any response beyond standard acknowledgment.

5. Selection and Award

5.1 Selection Criteria

Scholarship recipients are selected based on the published Selection Criteria. Selection decisions are made at the sole discretion of NaijaAssets or the designated selection committee.

5.2 Selection Process

The selection process may involve:

  • Initial screening and eligibility verification
  • Review and evaluation by a selection panel
  • Interviews or assessments (where applicable)
  • Final approval by NaijaAssets or authorized representatives

5.3 Selection Discretion

NaijaAssets retains full discretion in selecting recipients, determining award amounts, and imposing conditions. Selection decisions are final and binding, unless otherwise stated.

5.4 Right Not to Award

NaijaAssets reserves the right not to award any Scholarship in a given program if no applicant meets the Selection Criteria or if the program is modified or discontinued.

6. Scholarship Terms and Conditions

6.1 Award Terms

Scholarship awards are subject to the specific terms communicated to the recipient. Terms may include:

  • Award amount and payment schedule
  • Duration of the Scholarship
  • Ongoing eligibility requirements (e.g., maintaining grades, continued enrollment)
  • Limitations on use or application of funds

6.2 Acceptance

Scholarship recipients must accept the award within the specified timeframe. Failure to accept may result in forfeiture and reallocation to another applicant.

6.3 Non-Transferability

Scholarships are personal to the recipient and may not be transferred, assigned, sold, or exchanged.

6.4 No Cash Equivalents

Unless otherwise stated, Scholarships are applied to Platform Subscriptions or services and are not redeemable for cash.

7. Modification and Discontinuation

7.1 Program Changes

NaijaAssets may modify, suspend, or discontinue any Scholarship Program at any time, with or without notice, subject to applicable law.

7.2 Award Changes

We may modify, reduce, or terminate individual Scholarship awards if circumstances change, including but not limited to:

  • Changes in the recipient's eligibility or enrollment status
  • Violation of Platform policies or scholarship terms
  • Changes to the Scholarship Program
  • Error or misrepresentation in the application
  • Fraud, abuse, or misconduct

8. Taxes

Scholarship recipients are solely responsible for understanding and paying any applicable taxes on Scholarship awards. NaijaAssets may be required to report Scholarship awards to tax authorities. Recipients may be required to provide tax information and complete applicable forms.

9. Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is prohibited in connection with Scholarship Programs:

  • Submitting false, misleading, or fraudulent application information
  • Plagiarizing application materials
  • Colluding with others to manipulate selection outcomes
  • Offering or accepting bribes or improper incentives
  • Using automated tools to submit applications
  • Violating program-specific rules or eligibility requirements

10. Termination

10.1 Termination by Recipient

Scholarship recipients may decline or withdraw from a Scholarship at any time.

10.2 Termination by NaijaAssets

NaijaAssets may terminate or revoke a Scholarship award if:

  • The recipient violates these Scholarship Terms or Platform policies
  • The recipient provides false or misleading information
  • The recipient fails to meet ongoing eligibility requirements
  • The Scholarship Program is modified or discontinued
  • Fraud, abuse, or misconduct is discovered

10.3 Effect of Termination

Upon termination or revocation, any remaining Scholarship benefits are forfeited. NaijaAssets may seek recovery of amounts already disbursed in cases of fraud or misrepresentation.
