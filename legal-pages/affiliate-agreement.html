1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the meanings set forth in the Terms of Service unless otherwise defined below.

"Affiliate" means a User who has enrolled in the Affiliate program and promotes the Platform through referral links.

"Commission" means the compensation payable to an Affiliate for Qualifying Referrals, as determined by the then-current commission structure.

"Qualifying Referral" means a new User who: (a) registers for the Platform through an Affiliate's unique referral link; (b) activates a paid Subscription; and (c) satisfies any additional conditions established by NaijaAssets.

"Referral Link" means a unique URL or code assigned to an Affiliate for tracking referrals.

2. Eligibility and Enrollment

2.1 Enrollment

The Affiliate program is open to Users who meet eligibility criteria established by NaijaAssets. Enrollment is subject to application and approval. Approval is at our sole discretion and may be withdrawn at any time.

2.2 Eligibility

To be eligible, you must:

  • Have a valid account on the Platform
  • Provide accurate enrollment information
  • Comply with all applicable Platform policies
  • Not be located in a restricted jurisdiction

2.3 Multiple Accounts

Affiliates may maintain only one Affiliate account. Multiple accounts for the same individual or entity are prohibited.

3. Referral Tracking

3.1 Referral Links

NaijaAssets provides unique Referral Links or codes to Affiliates. Commissions are tracked based on referrals made through these links.

3.2 Attribution

A Qualifying Referral is attributed to the Affiliate whose Referral Link was used during the referral process. NaijaAssets' attribution and tracking records are final and binding.

3.3 Cookie Duration

Referral tracking cookies or similar mechanisms may have a limited duration as specified in the Affiliate dashboard. Referrals outside this window may not be attributed.

3.4 Self-Referral Prohibition

Self-referrals are strictly prohibited. Affiliates may not use their own Referral Link to earn Commissions on their own Subscriptions or purchases.

4. Commissions

4.1 Commission Structure

Commission rates, structures, and calculation methods are communicated through the Affiliate dashboard or other mechanisms. The current commission structure may be modified at any time with notice.

4.2 Qualifying Transactions

Commissions are earned only on Qualifying Referrals that meet all conditions. We reserve the right to determine what constitutes a Qualifying Referral and to modify qualifying criteria.

4.3 Commissions on Future Purchases

Unless otherwise specified, Commissions may be limited to initial Qualifying Referrals or may extend to future Subscription renewals, as determined by the then-current commission structure.

4.4 No Commission Guarantee

NaijaAssets does not guarantee any minimum Commission, number of referrals, or revenue. Affiliate earnings depend entirely on the Affiliate's promotional efforts and platform conditions.

5. Payment Terms

5.1 Payment Schedule

Commissions are paid according to the schedule published in the Affiliate dashboard. Payment is subject to minimum payout thresholds.

5.2 Payment Methods

Payments are made through available payment methods, which may include bank transfer, mobile money, platform credits, or other methods as determined by NaijaAssets. Payment methods may change over time.

5.3 Deductions

NaijaAssets may deduct applicable taxes, fees, or chargebacks from Commission payments as required by law or as specified in the commission structure.

5.4 Forfeiture

Commissions may be forfeited in cases of:

  • Fraud or abuse of the Affiliate program
  • Violation of this Agreement or Platform policies
  • Account termination
  • Refunds or chargebacks on referred Subscriptions
  • Failure to meet minimum payout thresholds within the specified period

5.5 No Guarantee

Payment of Commissions is contingent on NaijaAssets receiving payment from the referred User. Commissions on refunded, charged back, or fraudulent transactions will not be paid or will be deducted from future payments.

6. Prohibited Activities

Affiliates may not:

  • Use spam, unsolicited communications, or deceptive marketing practices
  • Bid on NaijaAssets trademarks in paid advertising without prior written approval
  • Misrepresent the Platform, its features, pricing, or value
  • Create websites or content that impersonate NaijaAssets
  • Use fraud, bots, fake accounts, or automated systems to generate referrals
  • Engage in cookie stuffing, forced clicks, or deceptive tracking methods
  • Promote the Platform in violation of any applicable law or regulation
  • Use Referral Links in illegal, obscene, or inappropriate contexts

7. Fraud and Abuse

7.1 Monitoring

NaijaAssets monitors Affiliate activity for fraud and abuse. We reserve the right to investigate any suspicious activity at our discretion.

7.2 Consequences of Fraud

Fraudulent activity, including but not limited to self-referrals, fake accounts, and artificial inflation of referrals, will result in:

  • Immediate termination of Affiliate status
  • Forfeiture of all earned and unpaid Commissions
  • Suspension or termination of the Affiliate's account
  • Legal action, including claims for damages and recovery of amounts paid

7.3 Recovery

NaijaAssets reserves the right to deduct or recover Commissions paid on fraudulent or invalid referrals from current or future payments.

8. Intellectual Property

8.1 Limited License

NaijaAssets grants Affiliates a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use NaijaAssets trademarks, logos, and promotional materials solely for the purpose of promoting the Platform under this Agreement.

8.2 Restrictions

Affiliates may not modify NaijaAssets branding or use it in a manner that suggests endorsement, partnership, or employment beyond the Affiliate relationship.

8.3 Termination of License

This license terminates automatically upon termination of this Agreement. Affiliates must immediately cease all use of NaijaAssets branding and promotional materials.

9. Term and Termination

9.1 Term

This Agreement begins upon acceptance into the Affiliate program and continues until terminated.

9.2 Termination by Affiliate

Affiliates may terminate this Agreement at any time by ceasing promotional activities or requesting removal from the program.

9.3 Termination by NaijaAssets

NaijaAssets may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, with or without notice.

9.4 Effect of Termination

Upon termination: (a) all rights and licenses granted under this Agreement cease; (b) unpaid Commissions for valid Qualifying Referrals made before termination will be paid in accordance with payment terms; (c) Commissions on future referrals cease.

The arbitration shall be conducted in English by a single arbitrator in New Mexico.
