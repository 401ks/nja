1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser or application when you visit a website or use an online service. They enable the Platform to remember your preferences, authenticate your session, and collect usage data. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixel tags, local storage, and SDKs used in mobile applications.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Platform to function properly. They enable core features such as authentication, session management, security, and account access. The Platform cannot operate without these cookies.

2.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the Platform to remember your preferences, settings, and choices, such as language preferences, display settings, and saved login information. They enhance your experience but are not strictly necessary.

2.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies collect information about how you use the Platform, including pages visited, features used, time spent, errors encountered, and interaction patterns. This data helps us improve Platform performance, identify issues, and understand usage trends. Analytics services we may use include but are not limited to services provided by Google, Microsoft, and other analytics providers.

2.4 Advertising and Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements, measure marketing campaign effectiveness, and limit the number of times you see an advertisement. We may use these cookies on the Platform and through third-party advertising networks. Where permitted, these cookies may track your activity across websites and services.

2.5 AI and Personalization Cookies

These cookies support AI Features by enabling personalized learning experiences, content recommendations, adaptive learning paths, and session context for AI interactions.

3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: Verifying your identity and maintaining your logged-in session
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Performance: Monitoring and improving Platform speed, reliability, and functionality
  • Analytics: Understanding usage patterns and learning outcomes
  • Personalization: Customizing content, AI interactions, and learning recommendations
  • Preferences: Remembering your settings and choices
  • Marketing: Delivering relevant promotional content where permitted
  • AI Features: Maintaining session context and improving AI model performance

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party Service Providers on our behalf. These include:

  • Analytics Providers: To help us understand Platform usage
  • Payment Processors: To facilitate secure payment transactions
  • AI Service Providers: To enable AI Features and model processing
  • Authentication Providers: To support account login and identity verification
  • Infrastructure Providers: For content delivery, cloud services, and performance optimization

These third parties may use cookies subject to their own privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

5. Your Cookie Choices

5.1 Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through your browser settings. You can typically choose to block all cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive a notification when a cookie is set. Please consult your browser's help documentation for specific instructions.

5.2 Platform Preference Center

Where available, you may manage your cookie preferences through the Platform's cookie preference center, which allows you to opt in or out of non-essential cookie categories.

5.3 Mobile Applications

Mobile applications may use device identifiers and similar technologies for analytics and functionality. You may control certain tracking through your device settings.

5.4 Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable strictly necessary cookies, the Platform may not function properly. Disabling other cookies may affect certain features, personalization, and AI functionality.

5.5 Opt-Out Resources

You may opt out of certain third-party cookies through industry opt-out mechanisms such as:

  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) opt-out
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA) opt-out

6. Do Not Track Signals

The Platform may not respond to Do Not Track (DNT) signals due to the lack of a uniform industry standard. We will continue to monitor developments in this area and adjust our practices accordingly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Where material changes are made, we will notify you through the Platform or by other appropriate means.
