What is NaijaAssets?

NaijaAssets is an online tutoring platform offering live structured classes in Mathematics and Science for secondary school students.

How Classes Work

Subjects & Students

We currently offer structured tutoring in Mathematics and Science for JSS and SSS students.

Requirements

Any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a stable internet connection is sufficient.

Just Google Meet for joining live classes and WhatsApp for communication, schedules, and updates.
