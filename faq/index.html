Getting to know us.

NaijaAssets is an AI-first education platform offering live classes, recorded lessons, AI tutoring, and educational resources through monthly subscriptions. We serve students primarily in Nigeria and across Africa.

NaijaAssets is designed for primary and secondary school students (JSS and SSS levels), their parents, and independent teachers who want to reach more learners.

We combine live teaching with AI-powered tools (AI Tutor, AI Whiteboard, AI Voice) in an affordable monthly subscription model. Our platform also includes content rewards, scholarships, and an affiliate programme for growth.

Plans, payments, and policies.

Subscriptions are billed monthly and auto-renew. Plans include Subject Subscription (one subject), Rex (recordings for subscribed subjects), Rex Pro (all recordings for your grade), and Vault Pass (unlimited access to every recording).

We occasionally offer promotional periods. Check our pricing page for current offers. Your account remains active even after subscription expiration.

Yes. You can cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll retain access until the end of the current billing period.

You have a 5-day grace period to update your billing information. During this time, access continues. After 5 days, access is restricted until payment is resolved.

Subscriptions are non-refundable. However, we review exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis at our discretion.
